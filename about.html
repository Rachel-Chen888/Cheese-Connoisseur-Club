About Us

We are the Cheese Connoisseur Club, dedicated to exploring and appreciating the finest cheeses from around the world.

Our Mission

Our mission is to bring together cheese lovers to share knowledge, taste new varieties, and celebrate the art of cheesemaking and cheesetasting.

What We Do

  • Monthly cheese tasting events (Lactose Free options available)
  • Educational workshops on cheese varieties and pairings
  • Guest speakers from the cheese industry
  • Cheese-making classes
  • Field trips to local dairies and cheese producers
  • Annual Cheese Leaderboard ranking
